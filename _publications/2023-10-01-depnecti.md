This paper introduces a novel task focused on identifying correct parsing and semantic relations between components of compounds in Sanskrit. We released 4 newly annotated datasets of nested compounds with contextual sensitivity and proposed a model that achieved an absolute gain of 14.4 points (F1) over the current state-of-the-art (SOTA) system of Nested Named Entity Recognition (NNER).
